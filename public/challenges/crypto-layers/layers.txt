UNG Cyber Unit — Intercepted Transmission
==========================================

A signal was pulled off an open relay last night. Analysts split it into
two blocks before the link dropped. Both blocks are text — no exotic file
formats, no hidden data. Everything you need is printable ASCII.

--- BLOCK ONE -------------------------------------------------------------

UExPUkVGVVZSWVE=

--- BLOCK TWO -------------------------------------------------------------

FLBAZUCKMJPQVLZ

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Analyst notes:
- Block One looks like the kind of text you'd see in a URL or an email
  attachment — but that's just the outer wrapper.
- Block Two didn't yield to a straight substitution guess. Whoever sent it
  used a repeating key. The defense team's go-to keyword for anything
  "protective" is a good first guess.
