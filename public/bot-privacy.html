// Discord Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. Applies to the "CyberUnit @ UNG" Discord bot.

// What we collect

When you link your Discord account (via Discord's own OAuth sign-in, requesting only the identify scope), we receive and store exactly two pieces of Discord-provided information on your website account:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • Your Discord username (as of when you linked)

We do not request, receive, or store your email address, your list of servers, your presence/activity status, or anything else Discord's identify scope doesn't provide.

// What we don't collect

The bot does not read, log, or store the content of any message, direct message, or voice channel. It does not scan server membership or roles beyond what's needed to check permissions where relevant.

// How we use it

  • To match a bot command (like a progress-lookup) to the right website account.
  • To show that account's already-public-to-them progress info (module/streak stats, earned badges, leaderboard rank) back through the bot.
  • To @-mention you by Discord ID in a staff-only Kanban-board notification channel, if you're an instructor/admin account and a card gets assigned to you.

Your Discord ID and username are never shown to other members through the website itself — Discord-link status is visible only to you, on your own profile page.

// Sharing

We don't sell or share this information with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. It's used only within this website and bot. Our infrastructure providers — Cloudflare (hosting, database) and Discord itself (for the account-linking exchange and bot messages) — process it only as needed to run the service, under their own respective terms.

// Retention & your choices

Linked Discord info is kept only as long as your account stays linked. You can unlink at any time from your website profile — this immediately and permanently deletes your stored Discord ID, username, and link date from our database. Deleting your website account entirely removes the same information along with everything else tied to that account.

// Children's privacy

This service isn't directed at children, and relies on Discord's own minimum age requirements for anyone using Discord at all.

// Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the bot changes. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
