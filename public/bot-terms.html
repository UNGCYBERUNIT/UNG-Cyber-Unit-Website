// 1. What this is

The CyberUnit @ UNG Discord bot ("the bot") is a free, non-commercial tool built and operated by the UNG Cyber Unit, a student cybersecurity organization at the University of North Georgia. It's a companion to this website (ungcyberunit.org) and currently:

  • Lets a member link their Discord account to their website account via Discord's own OAuth sign-in flow, so bot commands can look up that member's progress.
  • Answers read-only progress-lookup commands (e.g. module/streak stats) for a linked account.
  • Posts Kanban board assignment/due-date notifications into a designated server channel, @-mentioning the assigned member when their account is linked.

By inviting the bot to a server, using its commands, or linking your Discord account, you agree to these terms.
